## Why I build in public

I write every guide so someone else can follow it, including the parts that
broke. Most homelab tutorials show the happy path. The useful stuff is in the
errors, the wrong turns, and the tests that caught what I missed.

If one of these saves you an afternoon of troubleshooting, it did its job.

## How these docs work

Each page follows the same format:

- **Summary**: problem, approach, and outcome in one table
- **Goal**: what I'm trying to do and why
- **Steps**: what I actually did, with screenshots
- **Verify**: how I proved it works
- **Lessons**: what broke and what I learned
